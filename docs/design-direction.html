CineStack design direction

A dark, poster-first look built on Mantine. Posters carry the colour; the interface around them stays quiet, with one accent for actions and ratings. Use the switches to compare the accent options and the light scheme. Posters here are placeholders.

Accent
App scheme
Dark by defaultPosters look best on a dark ground. Light stays available from a toggle in the header.
Posters, not text rowsSearch and lists become poster grids. The overview moves to the title page.
Search in the headerFinding a title is the main action, so the search box is on every page.
One accentUsed for the main button, the add control and star ratings. Status has its own four colours.
Poster-credit headingsA condensed face for page titles and the wordmark; a plain sans for everything else.

Search

Results as a poster grid. The plus button opens a small menu of your lists, and a toast confirms the add. Titles already on a list show a tick.
